1. About Vistora

Vistora is an online marketplace. We provide the platform where independent vendors list products and customers buy them. Unless a product page says otherwise, the product is sold by the vendor named on it, not by Vistora.

2. Using the site

  • You must be 18 or older, or use the site with a parent or guardian.
  • Give correct information when you register and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Do not misuse the site: no fraud, no hacking or scraping, no uploading harmful or illegal content, and no fake reviews.

3. Products, prices and availability

  • Vendors write their own descriptions and set their own prices. We work to keep information accurate but cannot promise it is always error-free.
  • Prices are in Indian rupees. Stock can run out, and a price or listing may change before you order.
  • If an order cannot be fulfilled because of a pricing error or lack of stock, we may cancel it and refund any payment.

4. Orders and payment

  • Your order is placed when you complete checkout, and each item is then packed and shipped by its vendor.
  • Online payments are processed by Razorpay. Cash on delivery may be offered for some orders.
  • Coupons follow their own rules (minimum order, expiry, usage limit) and cannot be exchanged for cash.

5. Shipping, returns and refunds

These are covered by our Shipping and Returns & Refunds pages, which form part of these terms.

6. Reviews

Only customers with a delivered order can review a product. Reviews must be honest and respectful. We may remove reviews that break these rules.

7. Terms for vendors

  • Vendors must apply and be approved before they can sell.
  • Vendors must list only products they have the right to sell, describe them honestly, keep stock up to date and ship orders on time.
  • Illegal, counterfeit, unsafe or restricted items are not allowed.
  • Vistora charges a flat 10% commission on each sale. The remaining amount is paid out to the vendor's registered bank account.
  • Vendors handle returns and refunds for their own products under our Returns policy.
  • We may pause or remove a vendor or listing that breaks these terms.
