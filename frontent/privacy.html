Who we are

Vistora is an online marketplace that connects customers with independent vendors. This policy explains how we handle your personal information. Our contact details are on the Contact page.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Order details: delivery address, items bought, order history, coupons used, wishlist and cart.
  • Vendor details: shop name, owner name, contact details, GSTIN, pickup address and bank account details for payouts.
  • Payment status: whether a payment succeeded. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay and are not stored by Vistora.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends, and the small amount of data we keep in your browser (see below).

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To process orders, payments, deliveries, returns and refunds.
  • To pay vendors and calculate commission.
  • To send order updates and answer your questions.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To improve the site and fix problems.

Who we share it with

  • Vendors: only what they need to fulfil your order: your name, delivery address, phone number and the items you bought.
  • Delivery partners: the details needed to deliver your parcel.
  • Payment provider: Razorpay processes online payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and browser storage

We store a sign-in token and your cart details in your browser so you stay signed in and your cart is remembered. Signing out removes the sign-in token. You can also clear this data from your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed for orders, returns, accounting and legal requirements. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information.
  • You can ask us to stop sending you updates that are not about your orders.
  • To make a request, use the Contact page. We may need to confirm it is really you.

Security

We use hashed passwords, sign-in tokens and access checks so customers, vendors and admins can only reach what they should. No website can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

Children

Vistora is not meant for children under 18. Please do not create an account if you are under 18 without a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
