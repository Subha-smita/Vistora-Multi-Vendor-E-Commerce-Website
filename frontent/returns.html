Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind or got the wrong item? Here is how returns and refunds work.

Return window

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery. The window is counted from the day the item is marked Delivered.

What can be returned

  • The item is unused, unwashed and in its original condition.
  • Original packaging, tags and accessories are included.
  • You have your order number.

What cannot be returned

  • Items made or personalised for you.
  • Opened beauty, skincare and personal-care products, for hygiene reasons (unless damaged or wrong on arrival).
  • Gift cards and downloadable items.
  • Items returned after the 7-day window, or damaged by use after delivery.

An item that arrives damaged, defective or different from the listing can always be reported, even in these categories.

How to return an item

  1. Sign in and open My orders, or write to us on the Contact page.
  2. Give your order number, the item name and the reason. For damaged or wrong items, attach clear photos.
  3. We confirm the return and tell you how to send the item back (pickup or drop-off, depending on your address).
  4. Pack the item securely with its original packaging.

  • Refunds start after the vendor receives and checks the returned item.
  • After we start the refund, your bank usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.
  • Shipping charges are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
  • Coupon discounts are adjusted: you get back what you actually paid for the returned item.

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel before the item is shipped. Contact us as early as possible.
  • Once an item has shipped, you can refuse delivery or return it after it arrives.
  • Cancelled orders paid online are refunded to the original payment method.

Exchanges

If you want a different size or colour, return the item and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the item you want.

Orders from several vendors

Each vendor handles returns for their own products. You can return items from one vendor and keep the rest of your order.

Your statutory consumer rights under Indian law are not affected by this policy.
