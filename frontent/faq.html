Ordering

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. An account lets you track orders, save a wishlist and download invoices. Create one in under a minute.

Can I buy from different vendors in one order?

Yes. Add products from any vendor to one cart and pay once. Each vendor ships their own items, so the parcels may arrive separately.

How do I use a coupon?

Enter the code in the coupon box at checkout and press Apply. The discount shows in your order summary. Each coupon has a minimum order value and an expiry date. See current codes on the Offers page.

Can I change my order after placing it?

You can cancel before the item ships. To change the address or the item, contact us quickly on the Contact page and we will ask the vendor if it is still possible.

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

You can pay online through Razorpay (UPI, cards and net banking) or choose cash on delivery where it is offered at checkout.

Is online payment safe?

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card or UPI details are entered on Razorpay's secure window and are never stored on Vistora.

My payment failed but money was deducted. What now?

Do not pay again. The amount is usually returned to your account automatically within 5 to 7 working days. If it is not, write to us with your order number and the payment reference.

Delivery

How long will delivery take?

Vendors dispatch within 1 to 2 working days, and standard delivery then takes 2 to 4 working days. See Shipping for details.

How do I track my order?

Sign in and open My orders. Each item shows Placed, Packed, Shipped or Delivered.

Is shipping free?

Standard shipping is free on orders above ₹999. For smaller orders, the charge is shown at checkout before you pay.

Returns and refunds

Can I return a product?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging. Some items, such as opened beauty products, cannot be returned. Read Returns & Refunds.

When will I get my refund?

After the vendor receives and checks the item, we start the refund. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 7 working days.

Account

I forgot my password.

Open Reset password and enter your email address.

How do I download my invoice?

Sign in, open My orders, choose the order and use the invoice download button.

Selling on Vistora

How do I become a vendor?

Apply here with your shop details and payout account. Our team reviews every application. Once approved, sign in again to reach your vendor dashboard.

What does it cost to sell?

There is a flat 10% commission on each sale. The rest is paid out to you.

Why is my application still pending?

Applications are reviewed by our team, usually within 2 working days. If it takes longer, write to us on the Contact page.
