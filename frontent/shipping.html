Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses across India. Enter your full address and PIN code at checkout. If we cannot deliver to your PIN code, you will see a message before you pay.

Shipping charges

  • Standard shipping is free on orders above ₹999.
  • For smaller orders, any shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • If express delivery is offered for your address, its charge is shown at checkout as a separate option.

Delivery time

StepUsual time
Vendor packs and dispatches your items1 to 2 working days after the order is placed
Standard delivery2 to 4 working days after dispatch
Express delivery (where offered)Next working day after dispatch
Remote areasMay take a few extra days

These are estimates, not guarantees. Public holidays, weather and courier delays can add time.

Orders from more than one vendor

Vistora is a marketplace. If your cart has products from different vendors, each vendor packs and ships their own items, so your order can arrive in separate parcels on different days. You pay once, and each item is tracked on its own.

Tracking your order

Each item moves through four steps: Placed, Packed, Shipped, Delivered. Sign in and open My orders to see where every item is.

Delivery attempts

  • Please make sure someone can receive the parcel at the address you give.
  • If delivery fails because the address is wrong or nobody is available after repeated attempts, the parcel may be returned to the vendor. We will contact you to arrange re-delivery or a refund, and the original shipping charge may not be refunded.

Damaged or wrong items

Check your parcel when it arrives. If an item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. See Returns & Refunds for what happens next.
